# Parnuan Software Engineer Intern — Take-Home

Welcome. This take-home is designed to evaluate how you think, scope, and build — not how polished your final product is.

---

## What You'll Do

There are **two assignments** in this folder:

| # | Assignment                                                      | Status              | Focus                                   |
|---|-----------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| 1 | [Text → Transaction Flow](./assignment-1/ASSIGNMENT.md)         | **Required**        | Parsing, product inference, ambiguity   |
| 2 | [Memory / Learn from Corrections](./assignment-2/ASSIGNMENT.md) | **Optional (bonus)**| Personalization, data modeling, trust   |

- **Assignment 1 is mandatory.** Everyone must submit it.
- **Assignment 2 is optional.** It's a bonus signal — attempt it only if you have time after doing a good job on Assignment 1. A strong Assignment 1 alone is better than a weak 1 + 2.
- Assignment 2 naturally builds on Assignment 1. If you attempt it, you can reuse your parser.

## Expected Time

- **Assignment 1:** 1–3 hours
- **Assignment 2:** +1–3 hours (if attempted)

These are guidelines, not hard limits. We expect you to scope the work yourself — decide what to cut, what to go deep on, and what to leave as "would do next." **How you scope under a time budget is part of what we're evaluating.** If you spend significantly more or less, mention it in the README.

We would rather see one focused, well-reasoned submission than two rushed ones.

## What We're Looking For

- Clear reasoning from incomplete information
- Practical technical decisions under ambiguity
- Sensible scoping and honest trade-offs
- Simple, working implementation
- Good communication in the README

## What Usually Goes Wrong

- Focusing on UI polish instead of the core flow
- Overbuilding — abstractions, frameworks, "production" features
- Ignoring ambiguity instead of naming and handling it
- Skipping trade-offs in the README
- Burning all the time on Assignment 2 at the cost of Assignment 1
